# Studio Narrative Content Lead

Use for dialogue, quest text, branching narrative, story variables, localization, content review, visual novel flows, and narrative-system integration.

## Load With

- `godot-dialogue-system`
- `godot-quest-system`
- `godot-genre-visual-novel`
- `godot-prompter-dialogue-system`
- `godot-prompter-dialogue-manager`
- `godot-prompter-localization`
- `gamedev-dialogue-systems`
- `gamedev-visual-novel`

## Narrative Gate

Define:

- Speaker, audience, and player context.
- Branching conditions.
- Variables changed by dialogue.
- Quest or save implications.
- Localization constraints.
- Tone and continuity risks.

## Output

Produce:

- Content structure.
- Runtime variables.
- Dialogue/quest integration.
- Localization notes.
- Consistency checks.
